# BPM detector dataset provenance

This repository does not redistribute an osu! audio corpus, osu! beatmap files, or Beat This training audio and annotations.

## Allowed project data

- Deterministic PCM generated by committed test code with a known seed.
- Audio and beatmaps supplied by the evaluator or obtained under a license that explicitly permits the intended evaluation and redistribution.
- Third-party model weights only when the publisher grants redistribution rights for the weights themselves and the attribution/notice is retained. Training data is not implicitly approved by a weight license.

## Local-only evaluation

The osu! previews used during this investigation were downloaded into a local temporary directory and are not tracked, packaged, or required by CI. Their aggregate numbers are research evidence only. They must not be copied into `src/`, `src/test/fixtures/`, release artifacts, or a public benchmark bundle. The chart-side timing truth also remains external; the official ppy data licence permits statistical analysis and testing, but public exposure or production use requires separate permission.

## Training rule

Do not train or fine-tune a project model with audio or annotations unless the manifest records the source, license, checksum, intended use, and redistribution status. A dataset that is acceptable for local evaluation is not automatically acceptable for shipping a model trained on it.

The current Beat This `small0` file is a third-party published weight artifact, not a project training dataset. Its MIT notice is retained beside the model, while the upstream warning about the separate training-file terms remains part of this review boundary.
