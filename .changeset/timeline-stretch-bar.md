### 中文

**新功能**

- 进度条下方新增纵向拉伸条，可拖动调整时间轴每拍占用的纵向长度；该功能可在「编辑器 → 时间轴布局」设置中开启或关闭。

### English

**Features**

- Added a vertical stretch bar below the progress bar for adjusting how much vertical space each beat takes; it can be turned on or off under Editor → Timeline layout.
