### 中文

**新增**

- 事件新增对事件类型的修改：单选事件后，检查器顶部可在 X、Y、旋转、透明、速度、缩放 X、缩放 Y 之间直接迁移，数值保持不变；缩放类型保存到特殊层，特殊层禁用时不再列出，噪域线仍保留缩放类型。

### English

**Added**

- Events now support changing their event type: with one event selected, the inspector lets it move between X, Y, rotation, opacity, speed, scale X and scale Y while keeping its values. Scale types are stored on the extended layer and are hidden when that layer is disabled; noise lines keep them available.
