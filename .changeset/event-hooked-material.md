### 中文

**修复**

- 事件时间线现在用主题的钩定颜色绘制钩定事件条，钩定事件不再和普通事件使用同一种材质；长度为 0 的钩定事件不再被当作非法区间标红，也不再列入谱面问题面板。

### English

**Fixes**

- The event timeline now paints hooked event bars in the theme's hooked colour instead of sharing the ordinary event material; zero-length hooked events are no longer flagged as invalid ranges or listed in the chart issues panel.
